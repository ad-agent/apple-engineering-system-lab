# Design preservation

Pick a concrete fixture with enough content to scroll. Record its document identity, selection, scroll position, appearance, text size, locale and window size. Capture both endpoints before editing. For a recording, mark the frame where contact begins, the first visible displacement, reversal, release and rest. If contact or cancellation is invisible, record that uncertainty.

Track the same control across frames by its label, function, relative position and focus. Two similar shapes alone do not establish identity. Describe the observed displacement and ordering without assigning a spring constant. For a persistent toolbar, check whether focus, hit target and VoiceOver order survive its transformation.

After editing, repeat the fixture with the same settings. Align images at an unchanged landmark. Inspect differences outside the requested region and replay the original interaction as well as the new one. Check stored content after reopening.
