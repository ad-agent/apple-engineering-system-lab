# Interview notes (fictional, written for this challenge)

Six households, each interviewed once at home.

- "I put the shopping away and then I'd have to sit down with the app. I never do." (two adults, one child)
- "The milk is fine, it's the herbs and the bag of spinach that die at the back of the fridge." (couple)
- "I don't know the date. I know that chicken lasts a couple of days and rice lasts forever." (student flat)
- "When I'm in the shop I want to know if we already have eggs. That's it." (couple)
- "My partner ticks things off and I don't know if they used it or binned it. I'd like to know what we waste." (two
  adults)
- "I tapped the wrong thing and it was gone." (single adult; had tried a list app)

Patterns the interviewer noted: entry happens at the counter in one burst; nobody reads printed dates for produce;
"use soon" matters more than a full inventory; people use the app one-handed; two of the six use the largest text
sizes.
