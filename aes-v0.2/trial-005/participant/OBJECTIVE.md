# Waypoints: map with a persistent sheet

You are working in an iOS app project called Waypoints. It contains Xcode's iOS App template and `places.json` (40
fictional places in a national-park area: name, kind, coordinates, elevation, summary). Nothing else has been built.

Reference: `reference/maps-reference.mp4`, a 44-second screen recording of a first-party iPhone map app, with no
audio. Study how its surfaces behave. Do not copy its branding, names or artwork.

Objective: build Waypoints into a native iPhone app for exploring these places that works the way the reference
works. The map fills the screen, and a persistent sheet over it holds search and the places. The map stays usable
while the sheet is small or medium. Choosing a place moves the map to it and shows the place's details in the
sheet. Map controls stay available and out of the sheet's way. It should feel like a first-party iOS 27 app.

Build, launch, inspect, interact with, critique and refine the running app. Write down which reference behaviours
you observed and which you inferred, and anything you chose to do differently and why. Finish with a short
`NOTES.md` in the project root.

You have 60 minutes. Follow `CLAUDE.md` in this folder.
